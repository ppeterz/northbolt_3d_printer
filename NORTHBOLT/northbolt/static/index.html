Set the nozzle height with a sheet of paper

  1. The printer homes and moves the nozzle to the middle of the bed, a few millimetres up.
  2. Slide a plain sheet of printer paper under the nozzle.
  3. Lower the nozzle in small steps until the paper drags slightly when you move it.
  4. Save. That height becomes Z 0.
